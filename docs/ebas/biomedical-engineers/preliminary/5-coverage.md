# 5. Coverage

**5.1** This Agreement covers:

- **(a)** the Employers listed in [Appendix 1](/ebas/biomedical-engineers/appendices/1-list-of-employers) of this Agreement;
- **(b)** all Employees (as defined in [subclause 4.5](/ebas/biomedical-engineers/preliminary/4-definitions)); and
- **(c)** the Union if it is named by the Commission as an employee organisation covered by the Agreement (in accordance with section 183 of the Act).

Plain language terms: scope, who is covered.
