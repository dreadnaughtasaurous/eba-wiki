# 12. Worker Wellbeing and Employment Support Officers

**12.1** The following roles must be classified no lower than Grade 3 under Part 1 or Part 2 of [Schedule 3D](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3d-classification-structure-managers-and-administrative-workers) (as applicable):
- **(a)** Worker Wellbeing Officers, in accordance with [Schedule 3F](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3f-worker-wellbeing-officer);
- **(b)** Aboriginal Employment Support Officers, in accordance with [Schedule 3G](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3g-aboriginal-employment-support-officer);
- **(c)** Disability Employment Support Officers, in accordance with [Schedule 3H](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3h-disability-employment-support-officer); and
- **(d)** Veteran Employment Support Officers, in accordance with [Schedule 3I](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3i-veteran-employment-support-officer).
