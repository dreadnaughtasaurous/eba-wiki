# 5. Weekend Work

**5.1** All rostered time of ordinary duty performed between midnight on Friday and Midnight on Sunday will be paid for at the rate of 150% (based on 1/38th of the weekly salary set out in [Schedule 3B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3b-wage-rates-managers-and-administrative-workers))

**5.2** Where Employees are required to carry out duties on a Saturday or Sunday in excess of the weeks work, such duties will be paid for at the rate of 200% (based on 1/38th of the weekly salary set out in [Schedule 3B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/3b-wage-rates-managers-and-administrative-workers)).
