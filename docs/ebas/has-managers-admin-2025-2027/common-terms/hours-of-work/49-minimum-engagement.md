# 49. Minimum Engagement

**49.1** Each Employee will be paid a minimum of three (3) hours per ordinary shift, with the exception of Employees:
- **(a)** eligible for payment of overtime in accordance with [clause 20](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/consultation-disputes/20-managing-conduct-and-performance) of Section 2 (Overtime); and,
- **(b)** working a period of make up time in accordance with [clause 22](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/employment/22-full-time-employment) of Section 2 (Make Up Time); and,
- **(c)** working a period of recall without the Employee having to return to their workplace in accordance with [clause 46.2](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/allowances/46-recall) of Section 1 (Recall – No Return To Workplace) and;
- **(d)** eligible for payment of overtime in accordance [clause 6](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/preliminary/6-definitions) of Section 3 (Overtime).

**49.2** No Employee will be paid less than the minimum hours of engagement.
