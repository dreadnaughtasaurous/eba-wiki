# 21. Types of Employment

**21.1** Employees under this Agreement may be employed in any one of the following employment categories:
- **(a)** full-time employment;
- **(b)** regular part-time employment; or
- **(c)** casual employment.

**21.2** At the time of engagement an Employer will inform each Employee of the terms of their engagement, and in particular whether they are to be full-time, regular part-time or casual.
