# 22. Full Time Employment

**22.1** A full-time Employee is one who is ready, willing and available to work a full week of 38 hours, or an average of 38 hours as per [clause 17](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/consultation-disputes/17-consultation) of Section 2 and [clause 4](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/preliminary/4-application-of-this-section) of Section 3 (Hours of Work) at the times and during the hours that are mutually agreed upon, or in the absence of such agreement, as prescribed by the Employer.

**22.2** Subject to the provisions of [clause 17](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/consultation-disputes/17-consultation) of Section 2 and [clause 4](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/preliminary/4-application-of-this-section) of Section 3 (Hours of Work) a full-time Employee will be paid the full weekly wage for their classification irrespective of the number of hours worked not exceeding 38, or an average of 38 per week.
