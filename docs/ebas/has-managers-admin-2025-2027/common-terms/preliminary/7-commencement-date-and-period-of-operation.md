# 7. Commencement Date and Period of Operation

**7.1** This Agreement will come into effect seven days after the date upon which it is approved by the Commission.

**7.2** The nominal expiry date of this Agreement is 31 December 2027.

**7.3** The Agreement will continue to operate after the nominal expiry date in accordance with the provisions of the Act.
