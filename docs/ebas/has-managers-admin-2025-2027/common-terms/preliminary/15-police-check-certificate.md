# 15. Police Check/Certificate

**15.1 Requirement to obtain and maintain Police Check/Certificate**

Where an Employee is required to possess a valid Police Check/Certificate in accordance with legislation, it is a condition of the Employee's employment that they are required to obtain and maintain their own valid Police Check/Certificate.

**15.2 Dispute**

In the event of a dispute as to whether an Employee is required to possess a valid Police Check/Certificate in accordance with legislation, it will be resolved in accordance with [clause 19](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/common-terms/consultation-disputes/19-dispute-resolution-procedure) (Dispute Resolution Procedure).
