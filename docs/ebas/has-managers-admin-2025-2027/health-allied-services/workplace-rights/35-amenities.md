# 35. Amenities

*This clause does not apply to Mobile Dental Assistants employed by OHV or Ballarat Health Service.*

**35.1** Dressing rooms, rest rooms, bathrooms or shower rooms and lunchrooms will be provided for non-resident Employees.

**35.2** Suitable, healthy accommodation will be provided for resident Employees.
