# 1. Application of Section 2

**1.1** The terms of Section 2 of this Agreement are additional terms specific to Health and Allied Services Employees and Dental Assistants (as defined below).

**1.2** For the avoidance of doubt, all entitlements and terms contained in Section 2 of this Agreement have no application to Managers and Administrative Workers (as defined in Section 3).

**1.3** References to clause numbers in this section refer to the applicable clause within this Section.
