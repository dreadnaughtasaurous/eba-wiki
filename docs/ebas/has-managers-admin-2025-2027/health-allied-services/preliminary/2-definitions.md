# 2. Definitions

In this Section 2:

**2.1 Base rate of pay** means the rate of pay payable to an Employee in accordance with [Schedule 2B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2b-wage-rates-health-allied-services) for his or her ordinary hours of work, but not including any incentive-based payments and bonuses, loadings, allowances, overtime, penalty rates or any other separately identifiable amounts.

**2.2 Dental Assistant** means an Employee classified in accordance with Part 2 of [Schedule 2D](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2d-classification-structure-health-allied-services) of this Agreement, and paid pursuant to Part 2 of [Schedule 2B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2b-wage-rates-health-allied-services).

**2.3 Employee** means a person employed by an Employer listed in Schedule A of this Agreement who is employed in any of the classifications set out in this Agreement, other than employees employed solely or predominantly in the provision of public mental health services.

**2.4 Experience** means experience at any such work in any workplace subject to this Agreement within the last five years, excluding any unpaid leave.

**2.5 Health and Allied Services Employee** means an Employee classified in accordance with Part 1 of [Schedule 2D](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2d-classification-structure-health-allied-services) of this Agreement, and paid pursuant to Part 1 of [Schedule 2B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2b-wage-rates-health-allied-services).
