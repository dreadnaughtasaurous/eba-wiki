# 10. Certificate Allowance – Pathology Technicians

Employees who have successfully completed a course entitled Certificate for Mortuary Technician conducted by RMIT, or its equivalent, will be paid a weekly a Certificate Allowance – Pathology Technician pursuant to Part 1 of [Schedule 2C](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2c-allowances-health-allied-services).
