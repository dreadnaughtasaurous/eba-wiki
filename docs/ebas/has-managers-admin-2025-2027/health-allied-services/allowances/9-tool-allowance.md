# 9. Tool Allowance

**9.1** Employees who are classified as chefs and cooks who are not supplied with the necessary tools to perform their duties by the Employer, will be paid a Tool Allowance per week pursuant to Part 1 of [Schedule 2C](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2c-allowances-health-allied-services).

**9.2** The Tool Allowance is compensation for the supply and maintenance of tools.
