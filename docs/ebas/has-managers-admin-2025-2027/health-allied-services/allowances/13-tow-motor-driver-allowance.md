# 13. Tow Motor Driver Allowance

Health and Allied Services Employees, other than those classified as Driver, operating a tow motor or tow loader or similar vehicle or a fork lift for a minimum of two hours in any one day will be paid a Tow Motor Driver Allowance pursuant to Part 1 of [Schedule 2C](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2c-allowances-health-allied-services).
