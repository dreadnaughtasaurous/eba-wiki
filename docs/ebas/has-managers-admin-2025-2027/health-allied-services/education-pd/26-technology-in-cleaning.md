# 26. Technology in cleaning

**26.1** The parties recognise the important role that Health and Allied Services Employees play in protecting patients’ welfare by preventing the spread of infection.

**26.2** To this end, the HWU supports attempts to increase standards in cleaning in the public health system. Subject to the terms of this agreement, the HWU will work constructively with the Employer to implement new technologies and practices in cleaning in order improve infection control.
