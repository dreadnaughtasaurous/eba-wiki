# 19. Weekend Work

**19.1** All rostered time of ordinary duty performed between midnight on Friday and Midnight on Sunday will be paid for at the rate of 150% (based on 1/38th of the weekly salary set out in [Schedule 2B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2b-wage-rates-health-allied-services)).

**19.2** Where the Saturday or Sunday work involves work in excess of the prescribed rostered hours, such work will be paid for at the rate of 200% (based on 1/38th of the weekly salary set out in [Schedule 2B](/ebas/has-managers-admin-2025-2027/schedules/2b-wage-rates-health-allied-services)).
