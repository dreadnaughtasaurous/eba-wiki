# 27. Transition of All Other Employees

**27.1** From the commencement of the Agreement, the classification ‘All Other Employees (not provided for elsewhere)’ (pay code KM18) will be removed.

**27.2** Employees on this classification must be reclassified to appropriate classifications with effect from 13 October 2022.

>**Note:** this is the date when the Victorian Government’s COVID-19 pandemic declaration ended.
