# 13. Tool Allowance (Chefs and Cooks Only)

**13.1** Employees who are classified as chefs and cooks who are not supplied with the necessary tools to perform their duties by the Employer, will be paid a Tool Allowance per week pursuant to Part 1 of [Schedule 2C](/ebas/archive/has-managers-admin-2021-2025/schedules/2c-allowances-health-allied-services).

**13.2** The Tool Allowance is compensation for the supply and maintenance of tools.

Plain language terms: chef tools, cook tools.
