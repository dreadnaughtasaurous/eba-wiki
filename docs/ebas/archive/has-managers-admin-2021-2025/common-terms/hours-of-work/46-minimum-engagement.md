# 46. Minimum Engagement

**46.1** Each Employee will be paid a minimum of three (3) hours, with the exception of Employees:

- **(a)** eligible for payment of overtime in accordance with [clause 25](/ebas/archive/has-managers-admin-2021-2025/health-allied-services/hours-of-work/25-overtime) of
- **(b)** working a period of make up time in accordance with [clause 27](/ebas/archive/has-managers-admin-2021-2025/health-allied-services/hours-of-work/27-make-up-time) of
- **(c)** working a period of telephone recall in accordance with [clause 44.6](/ebas/archive/has-managers-admin-2021-2025/health-allied-services/classification-staffing/44-introduction-of-lifestyle-assistant-and-complimentary) of Section 1 (Recall – Telephone Allowance) and;
- **(d)** eligible for payment of overtime in accordance [clause 10](/ebas/archive/has-managers-admin-2021-2025/managers-admin/hours-of-work/10-overtime) of Section 3 (Overtime).

**46.2** No Employee will be paid less than the minimum hours of engagement.

Plain language terms: minimum shift, minimum hours.
